Frequently Asked Questions

You Ask, We Answer

How does SmartHR's AI resume screening work? ⌄

SmartHR parses each resume and compares its skills and experience against the job description using semantic matching, then generates a transparent match score.

Is candidate information secure? ⌄

Yes. All candidate data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with access limited to authorized recruiters on each job.

Can recruiters override AI recommendations? ⌄

Recruiters always review and approve AI-ranked candidates before any hiring decision is made.

Can multiple recruiters collaborate? ⌄

Yes, teams can share job postings, leave notes on candidates, and track hiring progress together.

How does bias detection work? ⌄

The system flags ranking patterns that correlate with protected attributes and surfaces them to recruiters for review.

How is candidate ranking calculated? ⌄

Rankings combine semantic skill match, experience relevance, and role fit, with each factor shown in a breakdown.
